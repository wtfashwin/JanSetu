# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

JanSetu is public civic infrastructure. It is built for citizens across many languages, regions, income levels and levels of technical skill, and the project community should reflect that.

We are committed to a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, caste, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are context gaps, not bad intent.
- Focus criticism on the work, never on the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise, and move on.
- Prioritise what is best for the people who will rely on this infrastructure.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language in any form.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks; trolling; deliberate derailing.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## A note specific to this project

JanSetu handles civic information, and civic information is political in effect even when it is not political in intent. Disagreements about jurisdictions, place names, languages, and which authority is correct will arise.

Discuss these as **design questions with evidence**, not as identity or ideology. Where the project cannot honestly decide who is right, the architecture is built to *represent the disagreement* rather than resolve it — and the same standard applies to conversations here.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — repository, issues, pull requests, discussions — and when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **95757821+wtfashwin@users.noreply.github.com**. Reports are reviewed promptly and in confidence. The reporter's identity is not disclosed without their consent.

Maintainers may take any action they judge appropriate, including warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban from project spaces. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face consequences determined by other maintainers.
